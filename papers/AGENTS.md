# Paper subtree instructions

`papers/` is for mature, publication-facing survivor packages. Do not move speculative or weak early ideas here.

## Entry boundary

A paper package is justified only after the scientific mechanism survives bounded Train/Dev falsification and has a cohesive candidate claim set worth supporting formally.

Early prototypes and formal Idea selection remain under `research/`.

## Evidence discipline

Paper-stage work may include:

- strong recent baselines under matched semantics;
- decisive ablations and mechanism attribution;
- multiple seeds and statistical evidence;
- robustness and subgroup analyses;
- frozen confirmation/evaluation contracts;
- untouched evaluation only after the analysis plan is fixed;
- manuscript, figures, tables, reviews, and submission artifacts.

The protocol can be frozen while scientific claims still narrow in response to evidence. Do not preserve a storyline when full testing weakens it.

## Provenance

Publication-facing claims must link back to authoritative run records or source-bound aggregate evidence. Do not copy exploratory numbers into the manuscript without preserving their dataset, split, information budget, seed, checkpoint-selection rule, and source revision.

Novelty, closest-work, SOTA, and benchmark-comparability claims require primary-source verification.
