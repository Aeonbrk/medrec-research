# Decision-Relevant Papers — Exposure-Localized Safety Reset

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rough et al., *Predicting Inpatient Medication Orders From Electronic Health Record Data*, DOI `10.1002/cpt.1826` | 2020, Clinical Pharmacology & Therapeutics | closest task prior | Order-time prediction, pre-order-only EHR timeline, 990 normalized medications, next-10-minute multilabel target | Order-time formulation alone is not novel; safety-state semantics must carry the delta |
| Johnson et al., *MIMIC-IV, a freely accessible electronic health record dataset*, DOI `10.1038/s41597-022-01899-x` | 2023, Scientific Data | data/protocol anchor | Separates provider prescription/request from eMAR administration; documents eMAR linkage fields and rollout | Makes executed-active medication state mechanically observable in raw MIMIC-IV |
| MIMIC-IV on FHIR | current public resource | schema anchor | Maps `prescriptions`/`poe` to MedicationRequest and `emar`/`inputevents` to MedicationAdministration | Confirms request/administration are distinct resources with explicit medication semantics |
| van der Sijs et al., *Time-dependent Drug–Drug Interaction Alerts in Care Provider Order Entry*, DOI `10.1197/jamia.M2810` | 2009, JAMIA | clinical semantic anchor | Some DDIs depend on coadministration timing and can be prevented by separating administrations | Static pair co-membership can be an over-broad safety trigger |
| Phansalkar et al., *Criteria for assessing high-priority drug-drug interactions for clinical decision support in electronic health records*, DOI `10.1186/1472-6947-13-65` | 2013, BMC MIDM | clinical semantic anchor | DDI probability depends on context; administration timing and half-lives matter | Supports exposure-conditioned rather than unconditional pair risk |
| Wasylewicz et al., *Contextualized Drug–Drug Interaction Management Improves Clinical Utility Compared With Basic DDI Management*, DOI `10.1002/cpt.2624` | 2022, Clinical Pharmacology & Therapeutics | strongest contextual-DDI anchor | Context suppresses alerts when administrations are sufficiently spaced, one drug is stopped, course is short, or no alternative exists | Gives direct evidence that pairwise alerts require workflow/clinical applicability context |
| *Designing and evaluating contextualized drug–drug interaction algorithms* | 2021, JAMIA Open | exposure-definition anchor | Defines basic concomitant exposure using overlapping drug-exposure periods and adds contextual factors | Provides a conservative operational exposure concept for a data-admission gate |
| SafeRx-Agent, arXiv `2605.29146` | 2026 | closest fine-grained safe MedRec | ATC-L4 recommendation with knowledge-grounded DDI/contraindication verification | Granularity and verifier novelty are occupied; proposed route must be exposure-state-specific |
| KATMed, PMID `41621768` | 2026, Journal of Biomedical Informatics | closest differentiable safety prior | Drug-disease contraindication rules as differentiable training constraints | Rule-conditioned loss is prior art; dynamic executed exposure must be the new state variable |
| HeteroMed, PMID `41567975` | 2026 | closest dynamic MedRec prior | Temporal medication expansion/inheritance plus expected static DDI regularizer | Medication-change modeling is occupied; DDI applicability remains visit/set based in the searched description |
| GRAIN, arXiv `2608.00098` | 2026 | closest DDI-granularity prior | Moves safety to active-ingredient-level DDI | Ingredient granularity is occupied; temporal/executed applicability is the remaining axis |
