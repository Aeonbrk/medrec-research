# Semantic Admission Ledger — Safety-Preserving Substitution Structure

- **Idea**: `005-safety-substitution-structure`
- **Formal Run ID**: `gate-01-output-structure-signature-20260904-155810`
- **Protocol**: `experiments/semantic-admission-protocol.md` (Commit: `587e3f626cf8c5849553176f8f1fae3aa2eb0d84`)
- **Adjudication Mode**: Blinded evidence adjudication; support counts hidden during clinical evaluation
- **Evidence Hierarchy**:
  - **Tier A** (required for strict admission): government/national clinical guideline, major specialty-society guideline, authoritative formulary therapeutic interchange guideline.
  - **Tier B** (corroboration only): FDA / DailyMed approved indications, warnings, routes.
  - **Tier C** (identity and taxonomy only): WHO ATC, RxNorm.
- **Negative Control**: `NAIVE_SHARED_INDICATION` records shared approved indication per Tier-B evidence, but cannot determine admission.
