## Interpretation boundary

A terminated Idea closes the tested scientific formulation under its recorded information budget and controls. It does **not** establish a universal ban on the nouns used by that Idea. For example, failed graph, retrieval, interaction, longitudinal, or privileged-information formulations do not forbid a future architecture that uses one of those primitives inside a materially different mechanism.

New exploratory candidates should start in [`../prototypes/`](../prototypes/README.md). Promote a survivor to a new formal Idea only when a frozen protocol and stronger claim-support workflow are justified.
